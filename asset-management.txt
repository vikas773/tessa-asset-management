1. Secure Login & Authentication:
    > Login using Company ID and password to ensure only authorized users can access the system.
    > Supports future role-based access such as Admin, Manager, and Employee.

2. Role-Based Access Control:
    > Different users see only the information relevant to their role.
    > Admins manage the system, managers track assets, and employees view assigned assets only.

3. Employee Management:
    > Admins can easily add, edit, or deactivate employee records.
    > Employees are linked to departments for better asset organization.

4. Asset Registration:
    > Assets can be added with essential details like asset ID, category, purchase date, condition, and value.
    > The process is designed to be fast and simple to use.

5. Asset Allocation & Return:
    > Assets can be assigned to employees and marked as returned with a single action.
    > Reduces manual tracking and avoids confusion.

6. Asset Status Management:
    > Each asset clearly shows its status such as available, assigned, under maintenance, or retired.
    > Helps teams make quick and informed decisions.

7. Asset History & Audit Trail:
    > Every asset movement and update is automatically recorded.
    > Ensures accountability and supports audits.

8. Maintenance & Service Tracking:
    > Repair and maintenance details can be logged for each asset.
    > Helps prevent asset breakdowns and extend asset lifespan.

9. Search, Filter & Sorting:
    > Assets and employees can be quickly found using search, filters, and sorting options.
    > Improves daily usability for large asset lists.

10. Dashboard Overview:
     > A simple dashboard displays total, assigned, available, and maintenance assets at a glance.
     > Provides management with a clear system overview.

11. Reports & Data Export:
     > Summary reports can be generated and exported to Excel or PDF.
     > Supports management reviews and audits.

12. Notifications & Alerts:
     > Users can receive alerts for asset assignments, return reminders, or maintenance schedules.
     > Helps ensure timely actions.

13. Data Security & Backup:
     > All data is securely stored with regular backups.
     > Protects against data loss and unauthorized access.

14. Scalability & Future Expansion:
     > The system is designed to grow with the company.
     > Supports future features without restructuring the system.
